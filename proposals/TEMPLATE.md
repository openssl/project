# <Title>

*These sections are a starting point, not a required structure. Keep what is
useful, drop what is not, and add whatever the proposal needs. Delete this
note.*

## Problem

What is wrong today, and for whom. Be concrete: describe the situation that
prompted this, not an abstraction of it. If the problem is not felt by anyone
yet, say why it will be.

## Proposal

What to do. Enough detail that someone other than the author could carry it
out.

## Alternatives

What else was considered, and why it was not chosen. Include doing nothing.

## Impact

Who and what this affects: contributors, infrastructure, existing workflows,
other proposals or policies. Note anything that has to change elsewhere, such
as a policy repository.

## Open questions

Questions the proposal does not answer yet. Leaving these in is fine, and
better than pretending they are settled. Remove the section when it is empty.
